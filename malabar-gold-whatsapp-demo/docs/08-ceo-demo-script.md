## 6. ANTICIPATED CEO QUESTIONS & CRISP ANSWERS

**Q: Data privacy / DPDP Act?**
A: DPDP-compliant by design — explicit opt-in consent, purpose limitation, data residency in India, full audit trail, right to erasure. WhatsApp is end-to-end encrypted. We treat customer data as a trust asset, exactly like your price tag.

**Q: Will she stay on brand — does she sound like Malabar?**
A: Maya's persona is locked to your voice: warm, courteous, never pushy, the "brand of trust." Every response is guard-railed and reviewed weekly in the pilot. She always discloses she's an AI and offers a human.

**Q: Hallucination risk — what if she invents a price or a product?**
A: Maya answers **only** from your verified catalogue, live rate feed, and policy base — not from open-ended generation. Prices, SKUs, and rates are retrieved, not guessed. Anything outside scope routes to a human. We measure and report accuracy weekly.

**Q: Gold-rate accuracy — this is sensitive?**
A: She quotes the **One India One Gold Rate** from your live feed, timestamped and labelled "indicative," and confirms final price at purchase. She never gives financial advice — she shares product info and offers a gold-investment expert.

**Q: Does this cost human jobs?**
A: No — it **redeploys** them. Maya removes the ₹50 "where's my order" drudgery so consultants spend time on the ₹10 lakh bridal close. Pilot deliberately keeps humans in the loop on every high-value deal. It grows your selling capacity, not cuts it.

**Q: Integration effort — how hard, how disruptive?**
A: A focused **4-week integration**: WhatsApp Business (BSP), your CRM, catalogue feed, and rate feed. Pilot is ring-fenced to 2–3 markets — zero disruption to the rest of the estate.

**Q: Total cost / payback?**
A: ~₹4–5.5 crore Year-1, illustrative payback **under 2 months**, ROI ~6–8×, with huge margin of safety even on conservative assumptions.

**Q: How do we *know* the ROI — not just believe it?**
A: Every interaction is measured — conversion, AOV, deflection, lead-routing time, CSAT — against a clean baseline and a holdout group. Go/no-go is data-gated, not faith-based.

**Q: What stops a competitor copying this?**
A: The bot is the easy part. The **moat** is your 300 stores, your trust programs (One India One Gold Rate, Smart Buy, DGRP buyback), your NRI base, and the proprietary preference/CLV data Maya compounds every day. A copycat bot without that loop is a toy.
